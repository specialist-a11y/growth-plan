          Questions parents ask

          Good questions.

          the honest answers

          What ages is it for?

          It's made for school-age children and teens, roughly 5 to 19. The default routine covers school mornings, homework and chores, and you can change every task to suit your child.

          Give their date of birth and their age keeps itself up to date — on their birthday the dashboard reminds you that the routine was set up for a younger child.

          How does the free trial work?

          Every new account gets 15 days free on Standard or Pro, with every feature of that plan. When the 15 days are up, choose a plan to keep going. Start your 15 days whenever you're ready.

          If you don't choose a plan, your family's data is kept for 15 more days, so you can still choose one or download a copy. After that it's permanently deleted.

          Can I set deadlines for tasks?

          Yes. Give each group of tasks a time to be done by, such as the morning routine by 7:30 or homework by 8 pm, with different times for school days and days off.

          A task ticked late still shows as done, but earns no points and doesn't count towards the perfect-day bonus or streaks. Nothing is taken away. For sick days or trips, excuse the day and deadlines don't apply.

          What can my child spend points on?

          Two things. Real-world rewards you set and approve — extra screen time, choosing Friday's dinner, a sleepover — and the in-app store, where points buy 55 themes, 30 characters, avatar frames, name styles and stickers.

          The store spends points only. There is no real money in it, nothing is random, and nothing is hidden behind a surprise box. You can switch the store off entirely from your dashboard.

          What are the marks on my child's character?

          Milestones. A seven-day streak earns a spark, thirty perfect days a halo, a thousand points earned an aura, and forty school days of homework with none missed or late earns Legend.

          They can't be bought in the store — only earned — and once earned they're never taken away. A broken streak costs the streak, not the mark it already earned.

          Can my child change the tasks or approve their own rewards?

          No. Tasks, rewards, the timetable and approvals are behind your parent PIN. Your child can tick tasks and claim rewards, and that's all.

          Do I have to type the whole school timetable in?

          Not if you'd rather not. Download the CSV template from your dashboard, fill it in in any spreadsheet — day, subject, teacher, room, time — and import it. You can export what's there at any time and edit it the same way.

          The daily routine works the same way, so a routine can be set up in a spreadsheet and brought in whole.

          Can I get the homework record out?

          Yes. Export it as a spreadsheet — one row per day, saying whether homework was done on time, done late or not done, with your notes alongside. Useful for a parents' evening, or just for seeing the pattern over a term.

          Does it know our school holidays?

          For Barbados, yes: public holidays and school terms are built in, so school tasks don't appear on days off.

          In other countries, weekends count as days off and every weekday counts as a school day for now. You choose your country when you sign up.

          Do I have to press save or sync?

          No. Every change saves straight away and backs up to your account automatically. If you're offline, changes stay on the device and upload when you reconnect.

          I forgot my PIN or password. Now what?

          Reset your parent PIN with your account password. If you've forgotten the password too, we'll email you a reset code and a link. If your PIN is ever reset, your dashboard shows when it happened.

          What does it run on?

          Any modern browser, on phones, tablets and computers. The same account opens on all of them and everything stays in step.

          There's no app store download, and no app store account needed for your child.

          Can we put it on the phone like a normal app?

          Yes, and you don't need Google Play or the App Store. Open the tracker on the phone and tap Download as app, or use Keep it on the home screen here.

          On Android the browser offers to install it in one tap. On an iPhone, open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Either way it gets its own icon, opens full screen with no browser bar, and keeps working with no signal — handy on the bus.

          Does it work without internet?

          Yes. Once it has been opened on a device, the routine can be ticked off with no signal at all. Changes are saved on the device and upload to your account the moment there's a connection again.

          What happens if I cancel Pro?

          Switch to Standard to keep going with your first child. Your other children's data is kept for 30 days, so you can download it or resubscribe. After that it's permanently deleted.
