    🎁 Rewards & storewhat the points are worth, and what can be bought with them

    🎁 Rewards

    Game cards come in four sizes, from 1,000 points for a $10 card up to 7,500 for a $100 one, so there's something a few good weeks away and something worth months of effort. Buy whichever card your child's game store sells and enter the code together. Edit any reward to change its wording or price.

    🛍️ Points Store

    Your child can spend earned points on themes, avatars and small extras. Buying is instant and costs no real money. Real-world rewards still need your approval.
    ⏰ Deadlinesthe times tasks must be done by to earn points

    ⏰ Deadlines

    Give tasks a time to be done by. A task ticked late still shows as done, but earns no points and doesn't count towards the perfect-day bonus or streaks. Nothing is ever taken away. Leave a time empty for no deadline.
    🏫 School & childtimetable, name, age and country

    🏫 School & Timetable

    Your child's school and weekly timetable. Leave it empty if they don't have a fixed timetable.
    Copy the timetable out of a spreadsheet and paste it here, or choose a .csv file. Either layout works: one row per day, or a grid with the days across the top. Write a room or teacher in brackets, like Maths (Ms. K; Rm 4) — a semicolon, since a comma would start a new column.
    Or work in a spreadsheet. The template has one lesson per row: Day, Subject, Teacher, Room, Time. Only Day and Subject are needed — write Lunch or Break as the subject for a break.
    One task per row: Task, When, Emoji, Id. When is one of morning, chores, evening, laundry or weekly. Id is only there so an exported file can come back without losing the history behind each task — leave it blank for anything new.
